## Description: <br>
Queue a content brief for one of the user's b/cited topical clusters and present it when ready: target queries, outline, entities, questions to answer, word target and schema types. Counts against the plan's monthly brief allowance. Use when the user asks for a content brief, what to publish next, or how to close a topic gap. Needs the b/cited account connection with the brief permission. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>
