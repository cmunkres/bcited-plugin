## Description: <br>
Show b/cited's weekly citation panel for a leaderboard category: which sites ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity cited when asked the category's fixed buyer prompts. Use when the user asks who AI engines actually cite in a category, which vendors ChatGPT, Claude or Perplexity recommend, or wants engine output rather than a scanner score. <br>
