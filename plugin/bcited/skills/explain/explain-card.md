## Description: <br>
Explain the six Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) signals that decide whether AI engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity) cite a site: llms.txt, ai.txt, AI crawler stance in robots.txt, citation-friendly schema, E-E-A-T, and body copy clarity. Use when the user asks what AEO is, why a site is not being cited by AI, or how AI answer engines choose sources. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>
