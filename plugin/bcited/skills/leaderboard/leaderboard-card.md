## Description: <br>
Show one category of b/cited's public AEO readiness leaderboard: 15 curated domains ranked by weekly rule-based scan, with grade, overall score and AEO score. Use when the user asks who leads a software category for AI-citation readiness, how a vendor category compares, or names a category like CRM, project management or SEO tools. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>
