## Description: <br>
Look up one of the 180 domains on b/cited's public leaderboard: its rank, grade and scores, the AEO checks that fail, its weekly score history, and how often AI engines cited it on the category's prompts. Use when the user names a well-known vendor domain and asks how it ranks, how it is trending, or whether AI engines cite it. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>
